# Trace: structural recursion during OE establishment

## Record status

This is a trace of the present OE-establishment activity. It is not evidence from the Android translation project and is not yet an ecological pattern.

## Event

While establishing the new Operational Ecology, discussion of the repository's preservation and ingress structure became recursively dominant. The investigation began elaborating directories, records, checkpoints, anchors and templates before re-establishing the existing OE definitions that explain the role of documentation and ingress.

The repository remained empty during this discussion. No premature architecture had been committed.

## Observable movement

1. A need for cross-session reconstructability was recognised.
2. Candidate mechanisms for satisfying that need were considered.
3. Those mechanisms became objects of investigation.
4. Structural proposals accumulated and began to attract attention independently of the target procedure.
5. The investigation's observer position became increasingly coupled to the structure being devised to contain it.
6. The existing OE account of documentation and ingress was reintroduced.
7. The distinction between ecological function and its representation became visible again.
8. The investigation returned to establishing epistemic anchors before further structural decisions.

## Collapse pressure observed

The means intended to preserve reconstructability began displacing the activity of reconstructing the target.

The pressure did not require an explicit decision to change the subject. It arose through recursive attention: the representational apparatus became the terrain of investigation.

## Intervention and reorientation

The existing OE definition established documentation as a preservation surface of traces and ingress as position-specific access to the ecology. This provided an epistemic anchor independent of the current structural elaboration.

The investigation then distinguished:
- the existing OE definition;
- interpretations derived from that definition;
- observations made during this OE's establishment.

It also clarified the separate functions of sufficiency, adequacy and completeness.

## Result

The investigation proceeded to preserve the epistemic anchor and establish only the minimal ingress and continuation records needed to begin examining the translation project's procedure.

## Limits

This event demonstrates that the collapse pressure can arise during the establishment of an investigation intended to guard against it. It does not, by itself, establish a recurrent locus or general pattern. Further observations in other traversals would be needed to determine recurrence and boundary conditions.

The event is preserved as a trace, including its intervention and reorientation, rather than compressed into a prescriptive rule.
